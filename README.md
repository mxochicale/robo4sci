# Modernising Robotics Skills for Collaborative Science through In-House Infrastructure: Distributed Intelligence, Cloud Computing, and Sensor-Driven Discovery Across Networked Systems

## :eyeglasses: **Abstract** 
Robotics is increasingly positioned at the intersection of cyber-physical systems, data-driven intelligence, and distributed computation. 
However, the pathway from fundamental principles, signal processing, multi-sensor integration, control theory, reinforcement learning, and computer vision, to advanced paradigms such as large vision–action models and large-scale orchestration for scientific discovery remains technically demanding and fragmented across tools and infrastructure.
This spotlight talk presents a practical perspective on lowering these barriers through modern, distributed robotics infrastructures at ARC-UCL (including Unified-AI and Condenser) that integrate cloud computing, containerised environments, and networked sensor systems. 
We examine the challenges of operating in-house robotic system with ROS2 that require coordination across heterogeneous software dependencies, operating systems, sensor modalities, and communication protocols, as well as the orchestration of computing resources for training large language and foundation models across distributed environments.
We share insights from a hands-on hackathon conducted across two UCL departments, Advanced Research Computing (ARC) and Civil, Environmental and Geomatic Engineering (CEGE), demonstrating a distributed architecture connected via a Zero Overhead Network Protocol (Zenoh) router over a 10 GbE link. On the CEGE side, physical sensing systems including LiDAR, inertial measurement units, and cameras were integrated using ROS 2 and ROSBridge for real-time data streaming. On the ARC side, participants worked within containerised environments and virtual machines hosted on unified-ai and Condenser platforms, enabling scalable experimentation with ROS 2 Humble on Ubuntu 22.04.
We conclude with practical lessons on reproducibility, dependency management, and latency–bandwidth–cost trade-offs, and close with a call for collaboration to strengthen ROS 2 capability, distributed robotics infrastructure, and cross-faculty platforms for scientific discovery.
